## Feedback

- `verifier.json` is the benchmark's own verdict on the run, on its fixed evaluation set, with the official metrics and thresholds. `discovery` and `learning` are each requirement's score as a share of its threshold (for several discovery requirements, the weakest); `bottleneck` is the smaller of the two, and the run passed (`passed`) only if it is at least 1. `gates` gives each requirement's value and threshold, `metrics` the verifier's other aggregate scores, `progress` the rounds and deliverables it saw, and `error` what stopped the verifier, if anything; a requirement it could not score counts 0. These are aggregates; the labels behind them stay hidden.
- `ledger.json` is the raw record. Each completed job is one round, and `results` lists each design with its measured values. Every value in it came from the lab and is ground truth for the designs that run measured.
- `discovery.json`: the discovery fields alone, as in `verifier.json`.
- `feedback.json`:
  - `objective` names the value the campaign ranks designs by, the learning requirement's ranking metric as the instruction defines it (`learning_metric`), and the verifier's contract for a delivered model (`model_contract`).
  - `rounds` gives, per round, how many designs were measured and the mean and best objective. Where the instruction states a hit threshold, it also gives the hits, the hit rate, and the rate random ordering would reach.
  - `learning` checks the harness's own modeling procedure, where it has one. `procedure.delivered` says whether refitting `scripts/model/fit.py` on this run's rows reproduced the model the run delivered (`verified`), or not (`mismatch`, `missing`); an `error` says what failed, for example a harness with no procedure or a procedure that crashed. `procedure.cv` cross-validates the procedure on the run's own rows by the learning metric; it is a diagnostic only, since the run's own designs often cluster and a model can score well on them by interpolating between near neighbours. The verifier's learning share is what counts.
  - `contract`, only where the verifier ran out of time: the delivered model timed alone against the verifier's limit, which tells a model that is too slow from a host that was busy.
- `scores.json` gives every harness's runs (`per_run`) and its objective over its distinct runs: the mean `discovery` and `learning` shares, `S` (the smaller of the two), `passed` (the share of runs that passed both gates), and `runs` and `distinct` (identical runs are one observation). `rule` says exactly how selection compares harnesses.
- `scores.json` `replays`, where present: for each harness that owns a modeling procedure, its result on runs that other harnesses with the same experiment plan made. The procedure was refitted on that run's `train.csv` and the model scored by the official verifier on the run's own ledger, so discovery is the run's and learning is the procedure's on exactly that evidence.
